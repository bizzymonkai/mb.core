### Known

Known places, persons, and companies already have network records on existing open systems. MB does not invent them. It cites them.

White pages hold two kinds of known:

| Kind | Source |
|------|--------|
| Local | This identity, and the accounts, companies, persons, places, and groups it has signed as associated. |
| Network | Places, persons, and companies that already have records on existing open systems (OSI). Cited, not re-created. |

A client resolves a name from known before crawling. A network-known record can be linked to an identity by a signed association. Until that association exists, it is a public referent, not a child of any identity.
